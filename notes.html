Things worth reading twice.

A small collection of lines I return to. Each one has a person and a place attached.

“This is for everyone.”

Tim Berners-Lee London Olympic opening ceremony, 2012

“千里之行，始于足下。”

A journey of a thousand miles begins beneath your feet.

Laozi Tao Te Ching, chapter 64

“Simplicity is prerequisite for reliability.”

Edsger W. Dijkstra Selected Writings on Computing, 1982

“The best way out is always through.”

Robert Frost “A Servant to Servants,” 1914

“Less is more.”

Ludwig Mies van der Rohe Architectural principle, around 1928

“You can't use up creativity. The more you use, the more you have.”

Maya Angelou Attributed; widely quoted in interviews

A note on sources. Quotes are checked against the source named under each one. Where a line is widely attributed but no primary source is clear, I say so instead of inventing a date.
